Usage: rrdtool [options] command command_options
* fetch - fetch data out of an RRD

	rrdtool fetch filename.rrd CF
		[-r|--resolution resolution]
		[-s|--start start] [-e|--end end]
		[-a|--align-start]
		[-d|--daemon <address>]
