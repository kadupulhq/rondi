Usage: rrdtool [options] command command_options
* update - update an RRD

	rrdtool update filename
		[--template|-t ds-name:ds-name:...]
		[--skip-past-updates|-s]
		[--daemon|-d <address>]
		time|N:value[:value...]

		at-time@value[:value...]

		[ time:value[:value...] ..]
